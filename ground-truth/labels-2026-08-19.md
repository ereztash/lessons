## 3. Adjudications

Every place the mechanical signal required a human call, with the evidence that decided it.

**`serving` = yes — MATI.** `https://mati-alpha.vercel.app` in README, `vercel.json` present, two
deploy commits, and the product is addressed to a named real organization (מתי״א רג״ב) whose
manager's 30 decision answers are recorded in `docs/manager-decisions.md`. Counted as serving
**despite** PR#18 listing "watch one real מדריכה use it" (R1) as still open — deployment plus a
named recipient is the signal; observed usage is a stronger bar the rubric does not claim to test.

**`serving` = yes — anti-silo.** `https://anti-silo.vercel.app/` in README, `vercel.json`, eight
deploy commits, and four documents tied to a real engagement: `CONSULTANT_PILOT.md`,
`LAUNCH_READINESS.md`, `INVESTOR_BRIEF.md`, `DISTRIBUTION.md`.

**`serving` = no — Agent-Architect.** The closest call in the set. It ships `product/OFFER.md`
(a canonical client offer in Hebrew), `landing.html`, and `sample-report.md`. But
`fixtures/paid-beta-protocol.md` opens with *"Do not start this protocol until Mini-Fixture v0.5
passes"*, and `docs/confidence-ladder.md` puts product-core confidence at 76–82% against a
target of 85–90% before paid beta. The gate is written down and has not been passed. **Commercial
documents are not a consumer** — that distinction is the point of the signal.

**`serving` = no — ground-state-protocol, brain-healer-hub, agency-insight-analyzer.** Each has
exactly one deploy-flavoured commit, and in each case it is the Lovable bot's
`Update site info for publish`. Counting the publish button as a consumer would encode the H2
failure mode into the answer key.

**`serving` = no — COR-SYS.** No deployment config, no live URL; the README documents
`localhost:3000` only. It uses Supabase, which is a backend, not a consumer.

**`serving` = no — CRM_Google_ai.** A mirror of `ereztash/_crm`; its README's CI badge points at
the source repo. Any consumer belongs to the source. This is the repo the rubric and the
classifier disagree about most (see results §2).

**`work_sessions` = 1 — COR-SYS, borderline.** Its longest internal gap is exactly 7 days, sitting
on the clustering threshold. At a 6-day threshold it would read 2 sessions. The label does not
turn on it: `pr_merges` 13 ≥ 5 makes it `managed` either way.

**`work_sessions` = 1 — brain-healer-hub, corrected.** An earlier pass counted "2 distinct calendar
months" (2026-03, 2026-04) and would have called it sustained. Its maximum gap is 3 days — one
continuous stretch from 2026-03-30 to 2026-04-03 crossing a month boundary. This is why the rubric
counts returns after a break rather than calendar months.

**`review_acts` = 2 — Agent-Architect.** No review-named branch, so the mechanical grep returned 0.
Hand-counted from commit subjects: `857e0dd Audit fixes: close 7 logic gaps found by Haiku audit of
v0.5 pipeline` and `88d4bfc Address review feedback — close 4 gaps`. Both are review acts by any
reading; the grep pattern missed them because the branch was not named for a review.

**`days_since_commit` = 92 — ground-state-protocol, corrected.** An earlier figure of 105 days was
carried over from the May scan's dormancy field. Measured from git: last commit 2026-05-19, and it
produced **9 commits after** the 2026-05-12 scan. The label is unaffected (92 > 30), but the number
was wrong and is fixed here and in `CLAUDE.md`.

**`executable` = yes for all 10.** Two repos needed the rule fixed rather than an adjudication:
`Benchmark.ATS` (stdlib-only Python, no manifest — the first rule required a manifest) and
`All_Erez-s_Connections` (one source file — the first rule required ≥5). Both are plainly runnable;
the rule was measuring size, not runnability, and was rewritten. Consequence: this signal has no
negatives in the set and therefore no discriminative power here (rubric §5.2).

## 4. What is deliberately not labelled

`serving` does not mean *used*. It means something outside the repo is positioned to consume it.
Distinguishing deployed-and-used from deployed-and-ignored needs analytics or a user observation,
neither of which is in git. MATI's own open criterion R1 is exactly that stronger test.
