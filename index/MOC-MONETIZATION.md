# MOC — Monetization

> Cross-dimension view: which insights pass the monetization gate and why.

## Monetization Gate (5 criteria, need 4 to pass)

1. **Reusable** — generalizes to a 6th repo not in the dataset
2. **Defensible** — non-obvious, would be paid for by a freelancer or small-team builder
3. **Time-saving** — prevents ≥1 hour of rework per future session
4. **Encodable** — can become a skill/command/template (not just a blog post)
5. **Evidence-anchored** — observed in ≥2 of the 4 source repos

Failed insights → `/insights/_parking-lot.md`. Re-audit allowed after 1 phase.
