## 3. Two new instances of the same engine

Both fit `research/cross-repo/portfolio-as-one-mechanism.md` without adjustment.

**`pre-call` — "from the conversation to the proposal."** Discovery-call preparation and a proposal
that returns the client's pain and value in their own words, for sellers of automation services.
Live at `https://pre-call-swart.vercel.app/`. Its value engine rests on a quantifiable process —
time saved, hourly cost, defect rate — and a scope catalogue for one vertical, and the README says
plainly that for a service which does not quantify that way "the tool's central claim will not
work." Carries a proprietary licence, `api/` with `_guard.js` / `license.js` and tests,
`docs/stopping-rule.html`, `docs/action-contract-audit.md`, `docs/first-ten.md`.

Component map: intake = the discovery call · extraction = the words module · scoring = the
estimate/value engine · **gate = `stopping-rule`** · **authority = `action-contract-audit`** ·
packaging = the proposal · **the price is the product**.

**`proofminer` — "you know you are good; nobody else does."** Converts evidence a person already
owns into visible, measurable, compounding authority, reported as one headline number, the
*Visibility Gap*. `src/{core,engine,adapters,data,ui,i18n}`, `docs/{ARCHITECTURE,AUTHORITY,METHOD,
TELOS,PROD_VERIFICATION,UX}`, a vitest suite, and `skills/{app-orchestrator,vercel-app-builder}` —
agent skills shipped **inside the product repo**, which no other repo in the portfolio does.

Its `docs/AUTHORITY.md` is the authority-boundary component under its own name, in a fourth repo.

That takes the engine from nine instances to **eleven**.
