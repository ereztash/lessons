## 3. Corrections to the classifier itself

**F1 false negative (proposed sub-feature F1b).** F1 asks for a non-template production dependency as a
proxy for domain commitment. It fails for repos that implement the domain rather than import it:
`MATI` (3 deps, 958 lines of domain logic), `Agent-Architect` (`anthropic` + `pyyaml`; the product is
prompts and fixtures), `All_Erez-s_Connections` (hand-written Express server, no scaffold).
Proposed F1b: **≥300 lines under a non-framework source directory with no corresponding dependency.**
Not yet applied to the scores above — it needs a pass over the other 25 repos before it changes any tier.

**"AI tools" column is unsound where it reads "none detected".** See
`research/cross-repo/authorship-attribution.md`. `Benchmark.ATS` was the one case in this round where the
error was checkable and it was wrong. The six "none detected" repos not in session scope stay unverified —
they are not evidence of anything until both detectors are run against them.
